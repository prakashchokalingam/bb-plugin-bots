# Changelog

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added

- Choose an animated blob avatar with a shape and color, or use an emoji.
- Expand a bot in the rail to open recent threads and see their states.
- Edit, start a thread, or delete a bot from the rail.

### Changed

- Show relative times for thread activity.
- Require BB 0.48.1 or later.

### Fixed

- Preserve existing emoji avatars during the database upgrade.
